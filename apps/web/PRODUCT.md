# ServiceLoop web — product truth

- Audience: car owners replying to a service reminder, and workshop staff demoing the flow.
- Job: turn a reminder into a customer-approved appointment in one conversation. The workshop messages first; the customer never starts cold.
- Truths the UI must always show: simulated workshop, no real appointment; booking happens only after explicit approval of exact slot and price; times in Asia/Kolkata, stored UTC; WhatsApp not connected; voice-note transcription not yet wired (upload says so).
- Interpretation is labeled: live Meta models or `rules-demo`, never silently swapped.
- Success on this surface: a visitor books, changes, snoozes, or cancels in under a minute and can point at what the system decided and why.
